# Emergency Medical Triage & Clinical Intervention Protocols

## 1. Adult Intramuscular Epinephrine Protocol for Anaphylaxis
For adult patients (>= 30 kg body weight) presenting with acute anaphylaxis, the first-line emergency pharmacological intervention is intramuscular (IM) epinephrine at a dosage of 0.3 mg to 0.5 mg using a 1:1000 concentration solution (1 mg/mL, equivalent to 0.3 mL to 0.5 mL volume). The injection must be administered intramuscularly into the anterolateral aspect of the middle third of the vastus lateralis muscle (outer thigh). Subcutaneous or intravenous bolus administration of undiluted 1:1000 epinephrine is strictly contraindicated due to delayed absorption or fatal ventricular arrhythmias. If anaphylactic symptoms persist or recur, repeat the 0.3 mg to 0.5 mg IM epinephrine dose every 5 to 15 minutes as clinically indicated.

## 2. First-Degree and Superficial Thermal Burn Cooling Protocol
Immediate first-aid management of first-degree (superficial epidermal) and superficial partial-thickness thermal burns requires active cooling under gentle, cool running potable tap water at a temperature between 15°C and 25°C for a full duration of 20 minutes. Cooling irrigation remains clinically beneficial when initiated within 3 hours of the thermal injury. Clinicians and first responders must strictly avoid applying ice, iced water, hydrogel packs below 8°C, butter, or greasy ointments directly to the burn surface, as extreme cold induces profound dermal vasoconstriction, deepens tissue necrosis, and precipitates systemic hypothermia.

## 3. Manchester Triage System (MTS) Clinical Priority Categories
The emergency department utilizes the 5-level Manchester Triage System (MTS) to assign clinical priority and maximum permissible waiting times before physician evaluation. Category 1 is Red (Immediate resuscitation), carrying a target wait time of 0 minutes. Category 2 is Orange (Very Urgent), carrying a maximum wait time of 10 minutes. Category 3 is Yellow (Urgent), carrying a maximum wait time of 60 minutes. Category 4 is Green (Standard), carrying a maximum wait time of 120 minutes. Category 5 is Blue (Non-Urgent), carrying a maximum wait time of 240 minutes.
