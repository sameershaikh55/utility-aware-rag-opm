# ADA511 Course Syllabus & University Academic Policy

## 1. ADA511 Grading Criteria & Assessment Weights
The course ADA511 (Data Science & AI Prototyping) evaluates students through three mandatory weighted assessment components across the autumn semester. The Final Exam Prototyping Project accounts for 50% of the total course grade, the Midterm Technical Portfolio accounts for 30%, and the bi-weekly Laboratory Assignments account for the remaining 20%. To pass ADA511, a student must achieve a minimum overall weighted score of 60% and maintain at least 80% verified attendance across scheduled laboratory sessions.

## 2. Project Submission Deadlines & Team Formation Rules
All ADA511 Final Exam Prototyping Projects must be completed with a required student group size of 2 to 4 students; solo submissions or groups exceeding 4 members are not permitted without prior written exemption from the Course Coordinator. The final project submission deadline via the university Canvas portal is November 27 at 23:59 CET. Late submissions without an approved medical certificate incur a strict penalty of 10 percentage points per calendar day up to 72 hours, after which the submission receives 0 points.

## 3. Academic Credit Requirements & Workload Allocation
ADA511 carries an academic credit value of 7.5 ECTS (European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System) credits. Under university curriculum standards, 7.5 ECTS corresponds to an expected total student workload of 200 hours over the 15-week semester. This workload comprises 45 hours of lectures, 30 hours of supervised laboratory instruction, and 125 hours of independent study, literature review, and software prototyping for the group exam project.

## 4. Formal Grade Appeal & Re-Examination Procedures
Students who wish to contest their published assessment grade in ADA511 have the statutory right to file a formal written academic grade appeal. The formal appeal must be submitted through the digital Student Registry Portal within 21 calendar days of the official grade publication date. Once registered, the appeal package is assigned to an independent reassessment committee consisting of two external examiners who were not involved in the initial grading. The reassessment committee renders a binding final grade determination within 30 calendar days of the appeal filing.
