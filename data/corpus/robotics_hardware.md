# Robotics Hardware & Actuation Specification Manual

## 1. Dynamixel XM430-W350-T Smart Actuator Specifications
The Dynamixel XM430-W350-T is a high-performance networked smart servo actuator engineered for multi-DOF robotic manipulators and mobile quadruped platforms. The actuator requires a nominal operating voltage of 12.0V DC (with an allowable input voltage range of 10.0V to 14.8V DC). At the nominal 12.0V supply, the XM430-W350-T delivers a maximum stall torque of 4.1 N·m while drawing a stall current of 2.3 A, and achieves a no-load rotational speed of 46 RPM. The actuator integrates a contactless 12-bit absolute magnetic encoder (4096 pulses per revolution, 0.0879 degrees resolution) and an ARM Cortex-M3 microcontroller running Dynamixel Protocol 2.0.

## 2. Serial Bus Communication & Baud Rate Configuration
Communication with the Dynamixel XM430-W350-T bus occurs over a half-duplex TTL serial interface (or RS-485 on the W350-R variant) using an 8-N-1 frame format (8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit). The factory default baud rate is 57600 bps, and the internal register supports configurable baud rates from 9600 bps up to 4.5 Mbps. For real-time ROS 2 control loops operating at 250 Hz, the standard production bus baud rate is configured to 1 Mbps (1000000 bps) with the Return Delay Time register (Address 9) set to 20 microseconds to prevent packet collisions on daisy-chained buses of up to 12 servos.

## 3. ROS 2 Foxy Fitzroy QoS Sensor & Control Profiles
In our ROS 2 Foxy Fitzroy middleware architecture, Quality of Service (QoS) policies are strictly segregated between high-frequency exteroceptive sensors and critical kinematic state publishers. High-rate sensor streams—specifically 3D LiDAR point clouds (`/velodyne_points` at 20 Hz) and raw 6-axis IMU measurements (`/imu/data_raw` at 400 Hz)—must use the `SensorDataQoS` profile: `ReliabilityPolicy.BEST_EFFORT`, `DurabilityPolicy.VOLATILE`, and `HistoryPolicy.KEEP_LAST` with a queue depth of 5. Conversely, joint state feedback (`/joint_states`), actuator command topics, and static coordinate frame transforms (`/tf_static`) require `ReliabilityPolicy.RELIABLE` with `DurabilityPolicy.TRANSIENT_LOCAL` and a queue depth of 10 to guarantee zero dropped kinematic updates.

## 4. Hardware Emergency Stop (E-Stop) Safety Wiring
The robotic platform enforces a Category 3 PL d hardware Emergency Stop (E-stop) circuit compliant with ISO 13849-1. The E-stop loop consists of dual-channel Normally-Closed (NC) mushroom-head pushbuttons wired in series with the safety relay coil that controls the primary 12.0V actuator power bus contactor while leaving the 5.0V logic rail powered for encoder diagnostics. Pressing any E-stop button breaks the NC series loop and de-energizes the main actuator contactor within a maximum hardware response latency of 15 ms. Resetting the power bus requires manually twisting the latched E-stop button clockwise followed by pressing the dedicated blue illuminated hardware reset pushbutton on the safety junction box.
